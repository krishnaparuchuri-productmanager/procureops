## 1. How to Read This Matrix

For each category, find the row whose upper bound is greater than or equal to the requisition's total value. That row's role is the **minimum** approver tier required. A requisition may always be escalated to a higher tier than required; it may never be approved at a lower tier than required.

## 8. Aggregation Rule (Split-PO Prevention)

DOA thresholds apply to the **total business need**, not to individual purchase orders. If multiple POs to the same vendor, from the same requester, within a 14-day window, combine to a total that would require a higher tier than any individual PO reached on its own, the aggregated total governs. This applies retroactively — approving PO #2 at the Requester tier does not become valid just because PO #1 was already separately approved at the Requester tier.

## 9. Exceptions

Any purchase approved below the tier required by this matrix is a policy exception and requires the Exception and Override Process in the Procurement Policy Manual, Section 8 — CFO or CEO sign-off and a documented reason code. No exception may be self-approved.
