# Standard Contract Terms

**Document ID:** POL-TERMS-001
**Version:** 2026-06-01
**Status:** Active
**Owner:** Procurement Governance Office

*This document is synthetic content generated for the ProcureOps demo. It does not represent real contract language and must not be treated as legal advice.*

## 1. Payment Terms

Standard payment terms are Net 30 from invoice receipt. Raw Materials / Production Inputs purchase orders over $100,000 use Net 45. Any vendor contract specifying terms shorter than Net 15 requires Department Head sign-off, since it constrains cash flow.

## 2. Incoterms and Freight

Default shipping term is FOB Origin (buyer assumes freight cost and risk once goods leave the vendor's facility) unless the purchase order explicitly states otherwise. Freight costs not itemized on the original PO but appearing on the invoice must be reconciled against the vendor's quoted freight rate before payment; unexplained freight charges are a price-mismatch discrepancy.

## 3. Duty and Tax

Import duty, where applicable, is calculated on the declared customs value of goods and is a standard component of total landed cost alongside unit price, tax, and freight. A quote that omits duty for a known cross-border shipment is treated as an incomplete quote for comparison purposes.

## 4. Warranty

Standard warranty is 12 months from delivery for consumables and general equipment, and 24 months for capital equipment (single-line-item purchases over $25,000). Warranty terms shorter than these standards require Department Head approval and a documented reason.

## 5. Termination

Standard termination clause allows either party to terminate with 30 days' written notice. Contracts with less than 30 days' notice, or with early-termination penalties exceeding 10% of remaining contract value, require VP approval.

## 6. Late Delivery Penalty

Standard penalty for late delivery is 0.5% of PO value per week late, capped at 5% of total PO value. This is a contractual term, not an automatic invoice deduction — application requires a documented late-delivery discrepancy record.

## 7. Confidentiality and Data Handling

All vendor contracts include a standard confidentiality clause covering pricing, specifications, and any data shared during the engagement. Vendors handling any data classified above "public" must have a signed data-handling addendum on file before a PO referencing that engagement is issued.

## 8. Renewal

Standard contracts auto-renew for 12-month terms unless either party gives 60 days' written notice before the renewal date. Auto-renewal does not exempt a purchase from DOA review at the point a new PO is issued against the renewed contract.
